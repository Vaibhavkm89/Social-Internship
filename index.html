About AI Fairness

What is AI Fairness?

AI fairness means ensuring that artificial intelligence systems make unbiased decisions. When machine learning models are trained on historical data, they can accidentally learn human biases related to gender, race, age, or income.


Why is Fairness Important?

AI algorithms are currently used in important areas like hiring, loan approvals, healthcare, and admissions. If an AI model is biased, it might unfairly deny opportunities to certain groups of people. Testing for fairness helps developers build ethical and trustworthy AI systems.
